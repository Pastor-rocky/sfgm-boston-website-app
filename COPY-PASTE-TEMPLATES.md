# 📧 Copy & Paste These 4 Templates into EmailJS

## ⚡ Quick Steps:
1. Go to: https://dashboard.emailjs.com/admin/template
2. Click "Create New Template" for each one
3. Copy/paste the content below
4. Save and copy the Template ID
5. Share all 4 Template IDs with me!
