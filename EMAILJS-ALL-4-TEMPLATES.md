## How to Use These Templates:

1. **Go to**: https://dashboard.emailjs.com/admin/template
2. **Click**: "Create New Template"
3. **For each template above:**
   - Copy the **Template Name** → Paste in "Template Name" field
   - Copy the **To Email** → Paste in "To Email" field (or leave as {{to_email}})
   - Copy the **Subject** → Paste in "Subject" field
   - Copy the **Content** → Paste in "Content" field
   - Click **"Save"**
   - **Copy the Template ID** that appears (you'll need it!)

4. **Repeat for all 4 templates**
